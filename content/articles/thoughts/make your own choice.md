## Decision

About decision, I have made many wrong decisions before. Like playing video games day by day in university, so I wasted the most precious time. Like escaping from my true self and pretending to be okay.

Today is 10.14, 2026. I have been out of work for seven months. Actually, I don't know what to do. Since I came into the university, I hardly spent any time thinking. I have to think through a lot of thing right now, but it doesn't feel like the right time. No job, no paycheck, and overthinking just makes me anxious.

When I was about 16, I thought I understood certain things clearly. But over the past few years, I've still been learning these lessons through my own experience.

After months of gap, I even don't want to work, I know once I go back to work, everything will be the same as before: working late, and coming home with no energy or mood to do anything else. I know it's not a good choice.

I need to figure out what I really want to do, what I really want to be. May be this is the year I'm most likely to start becoming one of those great people(Still dreaming).

Wish me good luck.
