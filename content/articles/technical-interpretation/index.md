# Technical Interpretation

These articles are my takes on technology: not just how something works, but how I understand it, which trade-offs I care about, and where I think it is useful.

## Scope

- **Linux & Embedded Systems** — kernel mechanisms, system design, and engineering choices.
- **Shared-memory IPC** — communication design, performance, and concurrency trade-offs.
- **RISC-V** — architecture, development tools, and the ecosystem from a developer’s perspective.

## Reading guide

Each article starts with a question or a point of view, explains the reasoning with evidence, and ends with a conclusion and its limits. Basic concepts and reusable explanations are linked to [[knowledge/index|Knowledge]] rather than repeated here.
